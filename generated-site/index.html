Our Process

Crafted With Intention,
Roasted With Precision

At ROAST, coffee is more than a caffeine kick—it is a craft. We partner directly with sustainable micro-lot farms across the globe, bringing rare single-origin beans to our custom vintage roaster. Every batch is profiled with exacting temperature control to highlight natural sweetness, vibrant acidity, and complex aromatics.

01

Source

We travel to origin countries, building lasting relationships with farmers who share our commitment to quality and sustainability. Every bean is hand-selected from micro-lot farms.

02

Roast

Our custom vintage roaster allows precise temperature control. Each origin receives a bespoke roast profile, carefully developed to bring out its unique character and complexity.

03

Cup

Every batch undergoes rigorous cupping and quality evaluation. Only coffees that meet our exacting standards for flavor, aroma, and body make it to your cup.

04

Deliver

Freshly roasted, sealed at peak freshness, and shipped directly to your door. We roast to order so every bag is as vibrant and bright as the day it left the roaster.
